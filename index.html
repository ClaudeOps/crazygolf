Format

Front 9: own ball

Everyone plays their own ball. Take the best 3 scores on each hole.

Back 9: shamble

Any two players go to one drive and the other two go to another drive. Choose the pairings after the drives. Play your own ball in from there and take 3 scores.

CTP Team closest to pin

Holes 3, 6, 8, 11, 12 and 14. The team needs 2 balls on the green, and the collar counts as on. Must make par to count.

2ND Team second shot closest

Holes 7, 9, 16 and 18. You must make par for it to count.

Breakfast ball

One off the tee, and it must be played (no choosing). You can use it for the closest-to-pin holes.

Mulligan

One, usable anywhere. If you use it, you must play it. You can use it for the closest-to-pin holes.
