Cross Creek

Executive course, par 60 · $10 entry, small bills please · Own ball and shamble in every team

How to play

First two names on each team

Play their own ball throughout.

Second two names: shamble

On the par 3s, they each play solo. On the par 4s, they both drive and each play in from the best drive.

Par 3s

Count three scores (of four scores).

Par 4s

Count two scores (of four scores).
