Cross Creek

Executive course, par 60 · $10 entry, small bills appreciated · 3 formats, 6 holes each

The game

Your foursome is one team. On every hole it splits into two 2-person pairs, and both pairs play toward the same team score. Pick partners before the drives.

Holes 1–6

Alternate shot

Everyone drives, and each pair picks one drive. The partner whose drive wasn't picked hits next, then alternate until the ball is in.

Best 2 scores count
Holes 7–12

Shamble

Each pair picks one drive, then both partners play their own ball in from there.

Best 3 scores count
Holes 13–18

Scramble

Each pair picks one drive, then scramble in from there, picking the best shot each time.

Best 2 scores count

22-inch rule

Putt the ball in, or stop when it's within 22 inches of the hole.

Partner rotation

You must play with each person twice in each format. One way to do it, with players A, B, C and D, repeated in every 6-hole block:

Holes in each blockPairs
1st and 2ndA + B and C + D
3rd and 4thA + C and B + D
5th and 6thA + D and B + C

Moving tees

Fall back or fall forward: the whole foursome moves together, based on the best score of the two pairs. Tee boxes run from 1 (longest) to 4 (shortest).

74 and younger start on 3
75 and older start on 4
Par or birdieMove back to a longer tee next hole (3 → 2)
Bogey or worseMove up to a shorter tee next hole (3 → 4)

Mulligan
